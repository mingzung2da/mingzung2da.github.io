Following Arksey and O'Malley's (2005) methodological framework, this scoping study identified 18 studies involving 8,868 students, published in 15 peer-reviewed journals from 2024 to 2026. Thematic analysis revealed five core themes of learners' feedback perception: trust (n = 9), accuracy (n = 6), affectivity (n = 6), social-evaluative threat (n = 5), and cognitive load (n = 4).
